I want to vibe-code a telegram bot that makes shared paying during trips more easy.

### How I see it:

- You add this bot to your telegram chat and it automatically identifies the participants of the trip. No registration needed.
- The bot uses a system of trips. You start the trip make several payments during the it and then close the trip. The bot should compute the minimum number of transactions needed.
- The interaction with the bot should be as easy as possible.
- It would be great to add somme instrument to track who has already returned money and who hasn't. Maybe some reminders.
