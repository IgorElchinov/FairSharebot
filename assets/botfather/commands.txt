start - Show a welcome message
help - List all commands and examples
starttrip - Start a new trip in this chat
starttriptoken - Start a trip that settles automatically on-chain
pay - Record a payment (equal, exact, or weighted split)
cansel - Undo a payment (see /trip <id> for its id)
balance - Show current balances and a settlement preview
closetrip - Close the trip and settle up
trips - List past trips in this chat
trip - Show the full breakdown for a specific trip
walletbalance - Show your token-mode wallet's balance
linkwallet - Link your own wallet instead of the auto-created one
exportkey - Reveal your auto-created wallet's private key (DM only)
minttoken - Operator only: mint tokens to someone's wallet
